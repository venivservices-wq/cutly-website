Cookie Policy

Effective date November 23, 2026

1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Cutly AI uses cookies and browser storage on our website and in our browser-based video editor. It also explains how to manage what your browser stores. For information about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small records a website saves in your browser. Some expire when your browser session ends, while others remain until they expire or you remove them. Browsers also provide storage that websites can use to save settings, project records, and larger files on your device. Data saved in this storage can remain between visits, and your browser may clear it automatically.

3. What Cutly stores and why

Terms of Use. The editor records which version of the Terms you agreed to so you don’t have to accept it again on each visit. It saves that record in browser storage and in a cookie that can last up to one year.

Account sessions. When you sign in, your browser keeps the session details, including sign-in tokens, that let you return to the editor without signing in each time. If you clear Cutly’s site data, you may need to sign in again.

Saved projects and jobs. The editor saves your source media, project files, and edits in your browser so you can return to your work later. Short-lived job records help the editor reconnect to an edit that is still running or offer to restart an interrupted export.

Editor preferences. Browser storage keeps choices such as your timeline layout, export settings, and whether you’ve enabled Help improve Cutly. It also records which tips or notices you’ve already seen and saves information used to display your credits.

Troubleshooting. Where diagnostic tools are available, the editor keeps a limited log of recent errors and related technical details in your browser. Older entries are removed as new ones are added.

4. Third-party features

Sign-in providers. If you choose a third-party sign-in method such as Google, the provider may use cookies or similar technologies to verify your sign-in and protect your account. The provider’s own policies explain how it uses and retains that information.

Contact form. Our website loads an embedded form from Tally when you open the contact page. Tally may use browser storage or similar technologies to operate the form. See Tally’s Cookie Policy for details.

5. Managing site data

Your browser settings let you block cookies or delete the site data saved for the Cutly website or editor. Clearing the editor’s site data can sign you out, reset your preferences, and delete projects stored in that browser. Export your work or save any files you need before clearing that data.

Files you downloaded or saved separately remain on your device. To request deletion of personal information held by Cutly, see Retention and account deletion in our Privacy Policy.

Where applicable law requires consent for a cookie or similar technology, we ask for your consent before using it and provide a way to withdraw that consent.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy if the technologies we use or the purposes for which we use them change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

If you have questions about cookies or browser storage, email support@cutlyai.app or use our contact form.
