Privacy Policy

Effective date November 23, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cutly AI (“Cutly”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal information when you use the Cutly video editor, visit our website, or contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, email support@cutlyai.app or use our contact form. You should also read our Terms of Use, which set out the conditions for using Cutly AI and its related services.

2. Your videos and projects

What stays on your device. When you import a video, the full source file stays on your device. Cutly saves your project in your browser’s storage. When you export, your browser renders the finished video and saves it to your device.

We do not keep copies of your source videos in Cutly’s storage. When you request transcription, we process the extracted audio temporarily and do not keep the audio file after processing.

Data sent for processing. When you request transcription, the editor sends audio extracted from the video for speech-to-text processing. For an AI edit or a follow-up request, Cutly may send your transcript, selected still frames, editing instructions, comments, and relevant settings to a processing provider. Still frames may show faces or other identifying details. Some editing operations also use timing, waveform, and other information derived from the project. Cutly uses these materials to process the request and return your results.

People in your material. Information about other people can appear in audio, images, transcripts, or written instructions. Before using a connected feature, make sure you have the necessary permissions or other authority to submit material about those people. Cutly’s editing tools may use cues such as face position or movement to help make editing decisions.

3. Other information we collect

Account information. When you create an account or sign in to Cutly AI, our video editor, we collect only the information needed to set up your account, verify your sign-in, and manage your access. The information we receive depends on how you sign in. For example, when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and an account identifier, and may receive your display name. We also keep records of your plan, credit balance and usage, and which version of the Terms you accepted and when.

Usage and security records. When your browser connects to Cutly, our systems and hosting providers may receive your IP address, browser or device information, request times, and technical information about requests and errors. We also record job activity, such as the type of operation, its status, when it ran, and credits charged or returned. We may use technical identifiers derived from requests or files to prevent duplicate processing, apply usage limits, and investigate abuse.

Purchases. Where paid purchases are available, the checkout provider handles payment details. Cutly may receive the information needed to record the transaction and apply it to your account, such as an order reference, amount, currency, payment status, plan, or number of credits purchased.

Support and feedback. We receive your contact details, messages, and any screenshots, files, or diagnostic reports you choose to include when you contact us or send feedback. Review attachments before sharing them, especially if they contain someone else’s information.

4. How we use your information

Provide the Services. We use account information to sign you in and manage access. We use the information sent with a connected editing request to perform the requested operation, return the result, and account for credits used. We also use information to administer any purchases and respond to support requests.

Protect and maintain Cutly. We use technical and account records to investigate errors, prevent fraud and abuse, protect accounts, enforce service limits, and resolve billing or account issues. If you send material to support, we may review it to understand and respond to the issue.

Evaluate and improve the editor. With your consent, we use feedback and other information you provide to assess how Cutly works, identify problems, and improve the Services. The separate option to share editing data for training is explained below.

Legal grounds for using your information. Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on why we use your information.

Our agreement with you. We use information as needed to manage your account, fulfil a purchase, or provide a feature you request. For example, we need your account identifier to manage your access and the audio from your video to provide a transcript.

Legitimate interests. We may use information to support users, operate and secure Cutly, prevent misuse, and handle disputes, provided your rights and freedoms do not override those interests.

Your consent. We rely on your consent when you agree to share feedback or other information to improve Cutly, choose to contribute editing data for training, or otherwise give consent where the law requires it.

Legal obligations. We process or retain information when applicable law requires us to do so.

5. Optional sharing for training

Your choice. Where available, Help improve Cutly is off by default. If you turn it on, Cutly may use the editing records you share to train and improve its editing tools. You can still use the editor with the setting off. Cutly records your choice so it can apply it to your account.

What is shared. While sharing is enabled, Cutly can receive a snapshot of the first AI edit and an updated snapshot when you export. These records may include transcript text and timings, edit decisions, captions, comments, editing instructions, and project details such as the filename, duration, and file size. The snapshots do not contain the source video, audio file, or finished export.

Turning off sharing and deleting training data. In Settings → Privacy, turn off Help improve Cutly to stop sharing new editing records. To remove records you’ve already shared, select Delete training data.

For help accessing or deleting your contributions, email support@cutlyai.app. For details on how we keep and delete account, billing, and support records, see Retention and account deletion.

6. Who receives your information

We share information with third-party providers that help operate and develop Cutly. What they receive and where they process your information depend on the service they provide.

Sign-in, hosting, and security. Sign-in providers process information needed to verify your login. Our account database and primary application hosting are based in the United States. The database holds records about your account, plan, credits, and job activity. Hosting and security providers receive technical information about requests, such as your IP address, and may process that information in other countries where they operate. Our providers include Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and account records, Google Cloud for application hosting, Cloudflare for domain and website infrastructure, and GitHub (Microsoft) for development and deployment. If you use another third-party provider to sign up or sign in, that provider processes the information needed to verify your sign-in under its own terms and privacy policy.

Transcription and AI editing. When you request a transcript, the speech-to-text provider receives audio extracted from your video. AI editing providers may receive transcripts, selected still frames, comments, and instructions needed for your edit. These providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. The terms and data processing arrangements that apply to each service determine how the provider handles and retains that information.

Storage for optional training. If you turn on Help improve Cutly, we store the editing records you choose to share in Cloudflare R2 in Eastern North America. See Optional sharing for training for what those records contain and how to delete them.

Support and contact forms. We use Tally for our contact form. Tally stores your contact details and message in Europe so we can respond. We may use Notion, Google Sheets, or similar tools to organise and track support requests. These tools may process your contact details, your message, and notes related to your request. If you contact us by email, your message may be processed in other countries.

Development and testing tools. We use third-party tools to develop, test, and maintain Cutly. Providers of these tools may process technical information, such as error reports and diagnostic logs, where needed to reproduce an issue, test a fix, or verify that a feature works as intended.

Payments. If you make a purchase, the checkout provider receives the billing and payment details needed to complete it. The provider may process those details in the countries where it operates. Its terms and privacy policy may also apply to your use of its checkout.

Other disclosures. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary and permitted by law to prevent fraud, secure the Services, protect rights, or handle legal claims. If you publish or share an export through another service, that service’s privacy policy applies to what you share with it.

7. Cookies and browser storage

Cutly uses browser storage to keep project files and editor preferences, maintain your sign-in session, and remember certain choices. The sign-in service and embedded contact form may also use browser storage or similar technologies to operate their features.

You can manage site data in your browser settings. Clearing it can sign you out, reset settings, and remove locally stored projects and edits. Export or save any project files you want to keep before clearing your browser data. Our Cookie Policy explains what the browser stores and how to manage it.

8. Retention and account deletion

Local projects. Projects you save in the browser editor are stored on your device. You can delete an individual project in Cutly or remove all locally saved projects by clearing Cutly’s site data in your browser. Your browser may also remove stored projects automatically. To remove downloaded exports or other copies you’ve saved separately, delete those files from your device. To delete personal information we hold, you can submit a request using the options below.

Editing requests and training contributions. We may retain the inputs and results of a connected job temporarily to complete it, deliver the result, or support a follow-up request. Processing providers may keep their copies for different periods under the applicable service terms. Separately, optional training contributions may remain while needed for the stated training purpose, subject to your deletion choices above and applicable law.

Account and support records. We retain account information while we need it to operate your account. We keep support, billing, and technical records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose they serve, taking into account the nature of the information and any legal obligations. Some records, such as transaction records, may need to be retained after account closure to comply with tax or accounting requirements or handle legal claims.

Requesting deletion. You can request deletion by emailing support@cutlyai.app or using our contact form. You can also use the account deletion option in the app where available. Some account information may remain while we process the request. We handle deletion requests within the time required by applicable law and retain only records we still have a lawful reason to keep.

9. How we protect your information

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections and controls intended to limit access to account records. Access to information is limited according to what is needed to operate, secure, and support the Services.

Keep your sign-in secure and protect access to the browser or device that stores your projects. If you believe someone has accessed your account or personal information without permission, contact support@cutlyai.app.

10. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to receive a portable copy, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it. Withdrawal applies to future processing based on that consent.

To exercise a right, email support@cutlyai.app or use our contact form. Tell us what you’re requesting and provide enough information to locate the relevant records. We may ask for information to verify your identity or authority to act for someone else. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Where available, Download my data in Settings → Privacy lets you download certain information associated with your account. Save media and projects from the browser separately if you want a copy of those files.

11. Age requirement

Our Terms of Use require users to be at least 18, or older if applicable law requires a higher minimum age. If you believe an underage person has created an account or provided personal information directly to Cutly, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action. For information about people who appear in a user’s media, see Your videos and projects.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Services or data practices change. The revised policy will show its effective date. We will give additional notice or request consent where applicable law requires it.

For privacy enquiries, requests, or questions about this Policy, email support@cutlyai.app or use our contact form.
